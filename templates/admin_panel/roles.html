Enterprise Multi-Tenant Security Policy

The platform operates on strict company-level data isolation. Company Admins and Users are restricted to querying, updating, and exporting only their own organization's records via foreign-key company locks on all Django ORM queries. Super Admins possess platform-wide observation and administrative capability while preserving audit trails for all configuration changes.
